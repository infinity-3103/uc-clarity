# UC Clarity: Product Prototype

An academic prototype for the course Business in Digital Age (SPJIMR Mumbai, PGDM 2026-28), Case 12: "Should Urban Company Turn Its Gig Workforce Into a Sales Force?".

The solution introduces **UC Clarity** as a transparent decision and financing layer inside Urban Company. When an appliance breaks down, the platform evaluates whether repair or replacement is more economical using explainable rules, neutral side-by-side comparisons, partner-bank no-cost EMI financing, and visual trade-in credits. Service professionals remain service professionals first, with an optional 3% referral fee on completed sales without quotas or penalties.

> **Disclaimer:** All data in this prototype is synthetic and generated by a seeded pseudo-random engine. Aarambh Bank and ReNew Partner are fictional entities created for academic demonstration. Urban Company and its Native product line are referenced solely in the context of the academic business case.

## Architecture and Shared State

- **Mobile Viewports (Customer & Professional):** Rendered within phone frames (390px by 844px) centered on screen.
- **Desktop Portals (UC Operations):** Enterprise multi-tab command dashboard for operations managers, regional demand forecasting, and compliance oversight.
- **Lender Architecture:** Aarambh Bank operates purely as a regulated backend API integration. Urban Company serves as the Lending Service Provider (LSP) and UI/UX layer without exposing third-party bank internal tooling.
- **Split View:** Shows customer and technician screens side by side so actions in one view reflect instantly in the other.
- **Shared Zustand Store:** Synchronized across views using local storage persistence with an instant `Reset demo` option.
- **Simulation Panel:** Adjust the simulated calendar date, active city, and outside ambient temperature (20 to 48 C) to trigger seasonal campaign nudges and hot-region demand spikes.

4. **EMI Selection & KFS Screen:** Customer selects "Buy new with no-cost EMI" (9 months). Bureau path approves instantly. Review the Key Fact Statement (KFS) where the acknowledgement checkbox starts un-ticked.
5. **Trade-In & Partner Referral Fee:** Trade-in credit is deducted and pickup requested to ReNew Partner. Imran referral tracker registers a 3% pending fee, which becomes PAID upon delivery.
6. **Lender Backend & UC Operations Oversight:** Aarambh Bank processes the loan via backend APIs. Switch to UC Operations view to monitor incoming applications, KFS disclosures, and disbursement conversions.
7. **Operations Funnel, Ledger & Economics:** Switch to UC Operations view. Examine the conversion funnel, consent audit ledger, and unit economics model reproducing the Case 12 benchmark table.
8. **Thin-File Path with Arjun (Account Aggregator):** Switch to Arjun Verma (Pune, thin-file). Book a refrigerator repair. Rahul Shinde inspects a compressor fault. Arjun applies for no-cost EMI; the Account Aggregator consent modal opens (consents start OFF). Upon granting consent, the cash-flow engine approves with FOIR between 0.40 and 0.45.
9. **Decline Branch & 10% Exit Survey Discount:** Arjun books a washing machine repair. Sanjay Patil inspects a motor fault. Arjun declines replacement and receives an optional 2-minute exit survey showing a 10% discount first. Completing it discounts the service fee.
10. **Campaign Console (Pre-Summer & Heatwave Nudges):** Set the date to 10 March and temperature to 41 C in Mumbai. Turn on MARKETING consent for Riya. Send pre-summer and heatwave nudges from the Campaign console and view them in her customer notifications.
11. **One-Click Data Deletion (Right to be Forgotten):** In Riya Consent Centre, click "Delete my data". Review the explanation of retained statutory loan records. Name updates to "Deleted user" across all dashboards and marketing nudges cease.

## Guardrails Implemented and Tested

- **G1 (Equal Prominence):** Repair and replacement cards share identical CSS classes, dimensions, and typography. No option is highlighted or pre-selected on load.
- **G2 (Repair Always Visible):** Repair cost and scope are displayed regardless of how heavily the scoring engine leans toward replacement.
- **G3 (Opt-in Partner Role):** Referral enrollment is strictly voluntary. Opting out carries zero penalties, ratings impacts, or quota demands.
- **G4 (Service Pay Parity):** Technicians receive the exact same service visit compensation regardless of whether the customer repairs or buys.
- **G5 (Allocation Neutrality):** Job assignment algorithms use travel time, skill match, rating, and SLA fit with zero weight assigned to conversion or sales.
- **G6 (No False Urgency):** No countdown timers, countdown banners, or pre-ticked consent/KFS checkboxes.
- **G7 (Unbundled Consents):** Four separate consents (Diagnostics, Marketing, Credit, Bank Sharing), each defaulting to OFF. Booking never requires marketing or credit consent.
- **G8 (Underwriting Privacy):** Credit underwriting strictly evaluates consented bank statements and credit bureau records. Phone contacts, media files, and location histories are never processed.
